RIVER HEIGHTS PRIVATE SCHOOL WEBSITE
=====================================

Project: E3812AT / E3852AT - Education Technology
Project 1: Develop a Functional School Website
Due date in brief: 18 September 2026

Included pages:
1. index.html — Home
2. about.html — About Us
3. academics.html — Academics & Activities
4. contact.html — Contact / Inquiry

Design:
- Navy blue and white school identity
- Custom River Heights crest/logo
- Responsive navigation
- Announcements / school news
- Mission and vision
- Faculty profile grid
- Curriculum table
- Extracurricular activity gallery
- Contact details
- Inquiry form with validation and email-client submission

IMPORTANT:
The school is fictional. Phone, email, address, staff names and event information are
sample content and should be replaced with the school's actual details before publishing.

HOW TO USE:
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser to preview the complete website.
3. Upload the files to a web host, or recreate the same pages in Google Sites/Wix/Weebly/
   WordPress.com/Canva Websites/Microsoft Sway as required by your course.
4. Before final submission, replace the sample contact details and connect the inquiry form
   to a real form service or school email.
5. Submit the published website URL/link as required by the assignment.

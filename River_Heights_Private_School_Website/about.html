About River Heights

Our purpose, people and values.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive and stimulating learning environment where every learner is supported to achieve academic success, develop strong character and become a responsible contributor to society.

Our Vision

To be a respected school community that develops confident, knowledgeable and ethical young people who are prepared to make a positive difference in a changing world.

“Learning today. Leading tomorrow.”

Our approach combines purposeful teaching with opportunities for learners to discover their strengths, work with others and take responsibility.

Our Core Values

Respect • Integrity • Excellence • Responsibility • Collaboration • Innovation
